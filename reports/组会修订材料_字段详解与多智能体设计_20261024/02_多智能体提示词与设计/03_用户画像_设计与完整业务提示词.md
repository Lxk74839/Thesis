# 用户画像

输入：可见历史评论、允许的商品属性，以及当次明确偏好。

输出：最多六项属性偏好，附权重、精确历史引用和正负立场。

设计：从评论中找真正有依据的偏好；没有依据可返回空画像。宿主继续检查引用和冲突，并按独立历史商品支持计算置信度。

下列业务提示须接在00号公共提示词之后，两者合起来才是完整system提示词。B0和I1按实验方法分别选用。

## I1已发送的多操作画像提示词

此版本保存了 12 次同类真实物理请求。示例请求标识：5b1d476719a43e02446cafac1788d9258d71980ec45f6735f3677a3adb4a8ae1。该示例来自多步特征运行，不代表该用户任务最终成功。

Analyze historical preferences ONLY, with exact source quotes. Do not rank or output current-request interpretation. I1 proposes at most six directly supported attribute preferences. Lover/loves/liked are explicit forms. Negation and conflicts require care; generic praise, incidental title words and unknown batch facts are unsupported. Current explicit preferences override history for that attribute; host normalizes weights/confidence. Return [] if no preference is supported. Unsupported proposals will be filtered, never invented to pass.
